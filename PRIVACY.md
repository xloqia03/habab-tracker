# Privacy Policy for Habab Tracker

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **Habab Tracker** handles your data.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Server Invites Data:** Information about invite codes, inviter IDs, and member join counts to operate the contest leaderboard.
- **Discord IDs:** Used strictly for tracking invites and displaying leaderboard rankings.

## 2. Data Usage
The collected data is used exclusively for managing community contests and invite tracking within authorized servers. We do not sell, share, or monetize your data.

## 3. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our official Discord server.
